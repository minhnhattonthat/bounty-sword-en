# Style reference - Bounty Sword

The voice every batch, fit pass, reviewer and the phase 9 story pass match against. Built from translated lines
seen in game (prologue warp, `work/qa/w_prologue6`, 2026-11-22) and from the phase 6 review. Terms and spellings
live in `glossary.md`; this file is about how people sound. Each row names the JP markers and the English devices
that carry them: a voice with no device on the page comes out neutral.

## Voices

| Who | JP markers | English devices | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| {hero} ({FF1F}, `<HERO>`) | オレ, terse plain form, だ/な endings, dry refusals | short sentences, contractions, understatement ("Sorry. I don't believe in gods.") | chatter, exclamation runs |
| Fairy ({FF28}) | わよ / わね / のよね / ～じゃない, はぁ, teasing | "you know", "huh?", "Oh, right", sighs ("Sigh..."), mild teasing, motherly warnings ("you'll be in big trouble later!") | stiff narration voice |
| Roger | ぼく(たち), です/ます to superiors, plain and hot to friends, ひきょうな！ | earnest, quick to anger, exclamation marks; contractions; "{hero}-san" | swearing, archaic words |
| Miranda | です/ます throughout, gentle | **no contractions** ("I would rather...", "I am so glad"), soft, measured | slang, "gonna" |
| Mauser | オレ様, ～ぞ, ～ぜ, うほッ, 出てきやがれ | third-person bragging: "the great Mauser", "the mighty Mauser"; "C'mon", "Whoa!?", loud boasts | a plain "I" where the JP has オレ様 |
| Tsukikage | ククク, シビれるぜ, ～だろ, アンタ, clipped | "Heh heh heh...", "Now that's a thrill!", "you" with an edge, short clauses | politeness |
| Colts | やれやれ, こいつぁ, しがない, laid back | "Good grief", "a bit of a pickle", wry and world-weary | heroics |
| Ajax (as ally, prologue) | オレたち, なあ, blunt | blunt, comradely, "Hey, {hero}!" | |
| Faust, Robun | Robun: ～ですな, ～ですぞ, どの; Faust: polite, scholarly | Robun: formal, a little old-fashioned ("It has been a long time, {hero}-dono."); Faust: precise | contractions for Robun |
| {FF31} (the princess) | ～じゃない, ～なぁ, warm then haughty | natural young woman's voice: "That was so mean!", "you know" | archaic royal register |
| Old men (ジジイ, 物知りジジイ, 村長) | じゃ, じゃろう, のう, わし | "I tell you", "my boy", "eh?", "hm?", rambling clauses | neutral modern lines |
| Old women (ババア) | ～じゃ, ～ですじゃ, おる | "dearie", "that is the rule", slow and folksy | |
| Elysion, Gauche, villains | キサマ, ～ぬ, ～がよい, 様を‥‥ | cold and grand: "So you've come...", "you wretch", "Stand there and watch", no contractions for Elysion | modern slang |
| Zodiac Gods, Sages | archaic ～じゃ/～である, 神の代理人 | the only archaic register (D9): "thou/thee" allowed, "agent of the gods" | jokes |
| Soldiers, captains | であります, 隊長, 様 | crisp military: "Sir!", "{hero}-sama", "Captain" | |

Honorifics (D1): -san / -sama / -dono / -chan / -kun stay on names and on {hero}/{FF31}, never "Sir {hero}" or
"Lady {FF31}" (glossary Rulings).

## Box fit (D10)

A JP box is one English paragraph of at most 3 lines x 158 px (~70-80 characters with the quotes). When the English
needs a fourth line, tighten it so it fits, or end the box at a sentence boundary: a box holding the last one to
three words of the sentence before it (a "widow", `batches.py widows`) costs the player a key press for nothing and
breaks the sentence. Dry-run with `python -X utf8 tools/batches.py fit '"page text"'`.

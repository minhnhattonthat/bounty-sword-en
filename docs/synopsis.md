# Synopsis (built from the extracted script; extend it as batches are translated)

No guide or wiki was used: everything here is read off `script/`. Mark a fact "(guess)" until a later batch confirms it.

## Who is who
- **Hero** (default name Sword, player-named: `{hero}`, speaker `{FF1F}`): a bounty hunter who once bore the title
  "Master of Swords" and threw the name away. Terse, dry, says little about his past.
- **The fairy** (speaker `{FF28}`, name word): his long-lived companion; gives the map hints ("This is East End...")
  and comments on every place. Chatty, teasing, jokes about being old ("I sound like a granny").
- **`{FF31}`**: a second variable name; the girl the hero meets at Crystal Cove and who is kidnapped by the Dark Lord
  at the Strait of Gibraltar (guess: the "Saint of the Frontier"). Also used for the client in job prompts.
- **Rebel army** (反乱軍): Roger (rowdy), Miranda (calm, polite; Roger's old friend), Robun, Mauser, Steyr,
  Colts, Hammerli, Detonics; first met in the Frozen Land.
- **Kingdom of Rheinmetall**: an island kingdom north of the continent of Europea (its port town is the only
  gateway to the continent); Ajax (general), the Minister of War. **Romulus** ("the royal city of Romulus") is a
  separate military power allied with Rheinmetall after the war ten years ago; the Federation destroyed it first.
- **The fairy is Thetis** ({FF28}; the hero calls her "Thetis" at the first Zodiac statue).
- **Rune** (Athena's older brother; speaks partly in a feminine register) commands the Federation's Valkyrie Corps;
  Athena (a Silver Knight of their fallen small homeland) swears to strike him down.
- **Ordoba Federation** (連邦): the expanding enemy power; soldiers, officers, airborne troops; uses "Machine God"
  ancient weapons late in the game.
- **Zodiac Gods** (黄道十二神): sealed powers; twelve rings (Ares Ring ... Poseidon Ring) to be gathered and bound
  into one; they name the hero their agent.
- **Villains**: the Dark Lord, Elysion, Gauche, Raggadol, Erebus and Nyx; Faust (a cultist chanting to dark gods,
  also scouted by the Federation).
- Others: Athena, Tsukikage (ninja), Ryubi (swordsman, "the Shredder"), Rune, Thetis, Norn, Macbeth, Daniel.

## Prologue (master_24DB56, translated b010-b011)
- Year 4095 of the Divine Calendar: the Divine Federation of Ordoba invades Europea from the south; seven nations
  fall; Rheinmetall (far north) is next.
- Ten years ago the hero, "the Undefeated Paladin" / "Master of Swords", fought in Rheinmetall's 17th Independent
  Knight Brigade with Ajax, Norn, Daniel and Macbeth; they beat the Zombie Master Raggadol ("all that awaits you is
  nothingness"). The fairy ({FF28}) was with him then (he had rescued her from confinement).
- The brigade learned it was a sacrificial decoy. Daniel left; Ajax dueled the hero to stop him going back to save
  the main force (scars: the hero's cheek, Ajax's left eye) and left; Norn went with the hero and died shielding him;
  the kingdom blamed the hero for the main force's loss. He drifted for ten years.
- Now: Thetis (his partner, takes the jobs, cheerful nagging) wakes him in a tavern: a job from the Rheinmetall
  knights via Minister Macbeth, wipe out the bandits in East End (100G up front).

## Mid game (master_1E0000, b012-b016)
- Map hints by Thetis for every location (b012-b013), then: the Ruins Town (the Fallen City) at Europea's southern
  tip, an evil spirit guarding "my father's legacy"; Faust reads ancient inscriptions about the gods' weapons and
  twelve children (Ordoba, Romulus, Rheinmetall, Iberia, Atlas, Rhipaean...) given the Zodiac rings to seal.
- Zodiac statues name the hero "agent of the gods": bind the twelve rings into one.
- Accra: Rune's Valkyries stall the rebels with Machine God No. 8. Aldabra Island: the fire dragon Salamander (the
  informer's job). Ship to the Isolated Isle (marines attack on the way): a 100,000-year-old boy holds the
  Poseidon Ring; with all twelve, go to the four sages of the Sage Village below the Rhipaean Mountains.

## Southern Europea and the castle (master_214606, master_257BD4; b017-b021)
- Forgotten Island: an old woman explains the dark wall around the Federation capital (only the legendary Sword of
  Bounty, given by the Zodiac Gods, breaks it; the hero's sword looks like it), Ruin Peninsula (a civilization that
  misused the gods' weapons, gone in one night), and the ultimate weapon (the Twelve Dark Gods bound into a god of
  destruction; Machine Gods are toys beside it).
- Lake Despair: Steyr kills the sniper captain who killed his sister Rossy at Romulus; Ryubi the Shredder (old
  comrade of Steyr the Flash) joins. South of it the Blood Shield (Leopard) falls ("the nation exists for its people").
- master_257BD4 opens with every spell/skill shout and combined attack (b020), then the castle assault: Machine God
  No. 7 (teleports), the Imperial Guard (a variant where recruitable allies fight for the Federation), and
  **Elysion, the Divine Emperor: {FF31} is his daughter**; her royal blood is the catalyst to revive the Machine God
  Supreme. He sends Machine God No. 12 (humanlike, close combat) and pins the party with his magic.

## Finale (master_257BD4 end, b022-b024)
- The party breaks Elysion's barrier on their own; Elysion is the evil spirit of an ancient people in the Emperor's
  body. He dies, but the Machine God Supreme still fails to wake; {FF31} (here also named **Furis** in narration)
  says her father acted of his own will. Then a voice: the Emperor's own death blood was the missing catalyst.
- The voice is **Minister Macbeth**, "agent of an evil god": he used the Emperor as a tool and had the hero find
  {FF31}; the Machine God Supreme levels the Rheinmetall capital (Colts and Hammerli die; Detonics survives
  briefly). Macbeth loved Norn and hated the hero for "taking" her. Every ally speaks; the hero: the gods were just
  people, the weapons are relics; "I fight for myself".
- Ending: the Zodiac Gods take back the rings and the Bounty Sword and sleep; {FF31} ("good girl"; the dog's-tail
  joke) joins the hero; each ally names their next road.

## Opening and early game (master_26F317 to master_280F1B, b025-b031)
- Crystal Cove (b031, the game's first job): Macbeth sends the hero to learn what the Federation seeks; it is
  {FF31}, washed ashore, aged 11-12, haughty ("bounty chaser"; calls Thetis a bug). With Colts, Hammerli (a woman)
  and the knight Detonics, they flee east through the Wolf Forest; in the capital Macbeth is away and {FF31} hires
  the hero herself (1000G to reach the port of Cherbourg).
- Rhipaean Mountains: Mauser the Dragon Buster (warrior of Oceanus, "face great evil with our fists") joins. The
  northern Silka fort: rebels Roger and Miranda call {FF31} "Princess" and ask the hero to lead them; base: Midgard
  tavern. Colts and Hammerli go home to Rheinmetall.
- Atlas Mountains: Captain Gauche, Roger and Miranda's old Federation commander, duels one of them and dies proud.
  Forgotten Valley: a former gravekeeper gives the key to the Sealed Tomb (north Rheinmetall), where Tsukikage
  the Ninja Master (hired against the hero) can be hired for 5000G. The Dark Wall at the royal city falls to the
  equipped Bounty Sword; a Federation guard lets them pass.

## Ship to Cherbourg and the road south (master_280F1B end, b033-b039)
- On the ship, Federation marines and their captain's roc Hiomaru attack; the dying captain begs "Princess" to come
  home. {FF31} reveals she is **Ophelia Les Elysion**, daughter of Elysion's eighth wife, sworn to kill her father.
  Cherbourg: the rebels are gone; the informer sends them to the Frozen Land (Silka fort ruins). The hero may refuse
  (on a whim) and come back (Thetis pushes him).
- Arden Forest: samurai captain Koho asks for a duel ("a knight is a knight if there's pride in his heart"). Ruined
  village (burned by the Federation; a boy guards his house for his mother). Erz plain ("Battlefield of Memories"):
  Daniel, now a Federation officer who envied the hero's name, dies in a duel.
- Midgard (secret rebel town) is attacked; the Dark Lord ("the Warlord") recognises the hero and leaves; the hero
  agrees to lead the rebels. Sage village: rings first; the sages send him to Highland (west of Rheinmetall) for the
  Bounty Sword; {FF31} is to be a sacrifice. Cape Iberia: airborne troops; Athena joins. Desert (Devil's Nest):
  Machine God No. 1 (fires on its own side), oasis people, No. 2 (anti-magic shield), Abadan River Corpse Immortal
  (Robun admits he seeks its art for his war-torn eastern homeland), Atlas Mountains (No. 3 controls allies).

## Game opening in Rheinmetall (master_290400, b040-b043)
- East End bandits (battle tutorial by Thetis: Deploy, Start Battle, A button, Retreat), Colts and Hammerli (a
  former nun) join at the tavern; jobs from the informer (Blackburn, frontier watch post overrun by undead, Wolf
  Forest: Federation platoon leader Taurus searches for something). Macbeth sends the hero to Crystal Cove with
  the knight Detonics. Optional areas: Eridanus River (Raggadol returns for revenge), Robun (feng shui) asks to join,
  Padova (monsters, Dark Dragon), Crimson Lake (a gods' trial).

## Late game (master_290400 end, master_2A7D44, b044-b050)
- Skull Knight guards a Zodiac statue and demands a one-on-one duel (each ally has a reply and a self-introduction).
  Ruined village: bandits (former villagers) beg for mercy; sparing or killing them, {FF31} reacts.
- Spania Plateau: the Federation Dragon Knights (they bombed Romulus first). Faust, the minstrel with the lyre,
  quizzes the hero (five choices) and joins as "the Ultra-Black Sorcerer". Highland: the Archsage's final trial
  (fight your own shadow), then the Bounty Sword; Rune (Athena's brother) attacks with Machine Gods No. 5, 9, 11,
  then joins. Road to the Federation through the Dark Wall; the Dark Lord at the castle is **Ajax** (lost his left
  eye to the hero; dies hoping to join Norn, or joins).
- 2A7D44: battle retreat lines for every unit, the ring shouts, the fall of Romulus, Steyr (Mobile Grenadier Corps,
  his sister's bullet necklace) joins, Sword Cape boatman (a letter from the Padova girl), the volcanic island
  (sorcerer general, the Shriek Whistle), Tartessos Gate and the three-headed Hydra, {FF31}'s "if I were a dog or
  a cat" night talk, Gibraltar (Erebus and Nyx), and the Dark Lord kidnaps {FF31} ("the princess is the key").
